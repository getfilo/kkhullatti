Biography

Academic Vision & Technical Specialization

Dr. Kirankumar Hullatti is a distinguished pharmaceutical academician with over 28 years of professional experience spanning industrial extraction, advanced phytochemistry, and institutional governance.

Specializing in Pharmacognosy and Herbal Drug Standardization, his work centers on bio-guided fractionation, HPLC fingerprinting, and standardizing botanical formulations for therapeutic application.

Core Competencies

  • Pharmacognosy & Phytochemistry
  • HPLC & Spectroscopy Fingerprinting
  • Industrial Natural Product Extraction
  • Anti-Diabetic Plant Screening
  • Ph.D. & PG Scholar Mentorship
