Biography

Academic Profile & Focus

Dr. Kirankumar Hullatti is a senior pharmaceutical academician with over 18 years of professional experience spanning teaching, research methodology, and institutional leadership. He holds a Master of Pharmacy (M. Pharm.), a Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences, and a Diploma in Pharmacy Practice Management (DPPM).

His primary domain of expertise centers on Pharmacognosy and Phytochemistry. His research focuses on the standardization of traditional herbal formulations, bio-guided isolation of active plant constituents, and quality evaluation using modern analytical instruments such as High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) and spectroscopic fingerprinting.

Throughout his career, Dr. Hullatti has actively mentored postgraduate scholars and Ph.D. candidates, directed research projects, and contributed to peer-reviewed scientific journals and patent applications related to natural product extraction techniques.
